OVERVIEW · slides 1–9

What this course is about

课程地图 · 两种定价方式

All of FM214 answers two questions: what is the price of risk? and what stops free lunches? · 全课就两问:风险的价格是多少?什么阻止免费午餐?

The two pillars 两根支柱
  • Equilibrium pricing (L1–5): start from investors' preferences, derive what expected return each risk should earn when markets clear. · 均衡定价:从偏好出发,推导市场出清时每种风险应得的预期收益。
  • No-arbitrage pricing (L6–10): say nothing about preferences; price assets off other assets' prices by ruling out free lunches. · 无套利定价:不碰偏好,用别的资产的价格、靠排除套利来定价。
  • They meet in one object — the stochastic discount factor 1=E[M(1+ri)]1=\mathbb{E}[M(1+r_i)]1=E[M(1+ri​)] — in L3 and L6. That equation is the spine of the course. · 两条线在随机折现因子里汇合,是全课的脊梁。

Measuring risk and portfolios

方差、协方差、分散化

Risk = variance. For a portfolio, mean is a weighted average but variance is quadratic and depends on every correlation — and that asymmetry is where diversification lives. · 风险用方差度量;组合的均值是线性加权,方差却是二次、依赖所有相关系数——分散化就藏在这个不对称里。

The frontier & two-fund separation

前沿、切点组合、两基金分离

Mean–variance investors trace a frontier (a "bullet"). Add a risk-free asset and the best menu is one straight line through the single tangency portfolio — so everyone holds the same risky portfolio, levered up or down. · 加入无风险资产后,最优菜单是过唯一切点组合的一条直线;于是人人持有同一个风险组合,只是杠杆不同。

Assumption (to be justified in L2) Mean–variance preferences: investors like higher E[rP]\mathbb{E}[r_P]E[rP​], dislike higher Var(rP)\mathrm{Var}(r_P)Var(rP​); nothing else about the distribution matters. Why only mean and variance (not skewness, crash risk)? L2 derives exactly when this is justified. · 均值—方差偏好:只在意均值和方差;为什么不管偏度?L2 会给出成立的确切条件。

Theorem · two-fund separation
With a risk-free asset, every mean–variance investor holds a combination of just two funds: rfr_frf​ and the tangency portfolio TTT. Investors who agree on μi,σi,ρij,rf\mu_i,\sigma_i,\rho_{ij},r_fμi​,σi​,ρij​,rf​ hold the same TTT. Risk aversion sets only the mix, never the composition of TTT.
  • Cautious: mostly rfr_frf​, a little TTT. Aggressive: levered TTT. Nobody holds a different risky portfolio. · 保守的多拿无风险、激进的加杠杆,但风险组合都是同一个 TTT。
  • Your job, age and tastes affect how much risk you take, not which risky portfolio. This is the intellectual foundation of index funds — and the springboard to the CAPM. · 这就是指数基金的理论根基,也是通往 CAPM 的跳板。

The CAPM

切点=市场 · beta · 证券市场线

If everyone holds the same TTT and markets clear, TTT must be the market MMM. Then what an asset must earn depends only on its beta — its covariance with the market, not its own variance. · 人人持同一个 TTT 且市场出清 ⇒ T=MT=MT=M;于是资产该赚多少只取决于它的 beta(与市场的协方差),与自身方差无关。

Equilibrium assumptions (stated honestly) all investors are MV optimizers over the same horizon; homogeneous expectations (agree on all μi,σi,ρij\mu_i,\sigma_i,\rho_{ij}μi​,σi​,ρij​); frictionless markets (no taxes/costs, unlimited borrow/lend at rfr_frf​, unlimited short sales). Strong — the value is the sharpness of the prediction; L5 confronts it with data. · 假设很强,但诚实摆出;模型的价值在于预测够锋利,L5 用数据检验。
Equilibrium: T=MT=MT=M By two-fund separation every investor's risky holdings are the same TTT. Markets must clear — all shares held by someone — so the aggregate risky portfolio — the value-weighted market portfolio MMM — must equal TTT. Prices adjust until the market portfolio is the tangency portfolio. · 价格会调整到市场组合就是切点组合。
Definition · beta βi=Cov(ri,rM)Var(rM)=ρiM σiσM.\beta_i=\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(r_i,r_M)}{\mathrm{Var}(r_M)}=\rho_{iM}\,\frac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_M}.βi​=Var(rM​)Cov(ri​,rM​)​=ρiM​σM​σi​​. Using the CAPM — and the data

例题 · 用途 · 实证

The CAPM gives the right discount rate for a given risk. But in the data, high-beta portfolios don't earn what the SML promises — the dots are too flat. Hold that thought for L5. · CAPM 给出风险对应的折现率;但数据里高 beta 并没赚到 SML 承诺的收益——留到 L5。

🟡 Example 5 · back out the market premium
A β=0.75\beta=0.75β=0.75 stock has expected excess return 6%. Find the market premium, then the total expected return of a β=1.3\beta=1.3β=1.3 stock when rf=2%r_f=2\%rf​=2%.

What is the CAPM for? · CAPM 有什么用

  • Discount rates: valuing a project/stock needs an expected return matched to its risk — rˉ=rf+β(E[rM]−rf)\bar r=r_f+\beta(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)rˉ=rf​+β(E[rM​]−rf​). Exactly the input L4's valuation formulas need. · 给估值提供折现率,正是 L4 要的输入。
  • Cost of capital: same formula from the firm's side. · 企业端就是资本成本。
  • Performance evaluation: skill is α\alphaα, not raw return — levering beta is not skill. · 本事是 alpha,不是堆 beta。
  • Portfolio tilting: found α>0\alpha>0α>0? Overweight it — but recognise you're disagreeing with the market. · 你在跟市场对赌。
Does the data support it? Ten β\betaβ-sorted US portfolios (1963–2026): the realised line is too flat — high-β\betaβ portfolios do not earn what the SML (slope ≈ 7.2) promises. The empirical failure, and what to make of it, is Lecture 5. · 按 beta 排序的组合实际收益线太平,高 beta 没赚到承诺的钱;完整说法在 L5。
