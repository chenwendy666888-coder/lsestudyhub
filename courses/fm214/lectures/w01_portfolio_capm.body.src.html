What this course is about

课程地图 · 两种定价方式

All of FM214 answers two questions: what is the price of risk? and what stops free lunches? · 全课就两问:风险的价格是多少?什么阻止免费午餐?

The two pillars 两根支柱
  • Equilibrium pricing (L1–5): start from investors' preferences, derive what expected return each risk should earn when markets clear. · 均衡定价:从偏好出发,推导市场出清时每种风险应得的预期收益。
  • No-arbitrage pricing (L6–10): say nothing about preferences; price assets off other assets' prices by ruling out free lunches. · 无套利定价:不碰偏好,用别的资产的价格、靠排除套利来定价。
  • They meet in one object — the stochastic discount factor \(1=\mathbb{E}[M(1+r_i)]\) — in L3 and L6. That equation is the spine of the course. · 两条线在随机折现因子里汇合,是全课的脊梁。

PART 1 · slides 10–13

Risk and return: look at the data

先看一个世纪的数据

Over 1926–2025, equities earned far more than bills — and swung far more. Two facts the whole lecture must explain. · 百年数据:股票收益远高、波动也远大;这两个事实是今天要解释的。

A dollar since 1926 · 1926 年的一美元

  • $1 at end-1925, income reinvested. Nominally equities end at $16,960, bills at $25; prices rose ~19×. · 名义上股票变成 \( \$16{,}960 \),国库券只有 \( \$25 \)。
  • Real (CPI-deflated): equities \(\to\$916\), bills \(\to\$1.34\). Bills barely beat inflation. · 实际(扣通胀)后国库券几乎没跑赢物价。

  • Equity premium: equities beat bills by 8.8%/yr arithmetic (7.0% compounded). 这是全课最重要的一个数字。
  • Higher average return comes with higher SD — the asset rows line up. · 平均收益越高、标准差越大,三行资产正好排成一列。
  • Arithmetic > geometric whenever returns are volatile, and the gap \(\approx \sigma^2/2\) (equities: \(12.1-10.2\approx19.7^2/2\div100\)). What compounds your wealth is the geometric mean — and equities still win. · 有波动时算术均值 > 几何均值,差约 \(\sigma^2/2\);真正复利你财富的是几何均值。
Two facts to explain 两个待解释的事实
  • Fact 1. Stocks earn more than bonds and bills on average.
  • Fact 2. Stocks are much riskier (1931: −44%; 1933: +57%; 2022: stocks −20% and 10y bonds −15%).
  • Natural hypothesis: (1) is compensation for (2) — a risk premium. · 自然猜想:高收益是对高风险的补偿。
  • But which risk, exactly? Total volatility, or something narrower? L1–L3 answer in three passes. · 但到底是哪种风险?这正是 L1–L3 分三步回答的。
PART 2 · slides 14–18

Measuring risk and portfolios

方差、协方差、分散化

Risk = variance. For a portfolio, mean is a weighted average but variance is quadratic and depends on every correlation — and that asymmetry is where diversification lives. · 风险用方差度量;组合的均值是线性加权,方差却是二次、依赖所有相关系数——分散化就藏在这个不对称里。

Definition · risk of a return For a random return \(r\) with mean \(\mu=\mathbb{E}[r]\): \[\mathrm{Var}(r)=\sigma^2=\mathbb{E}\!\left[(r-\mu)^2\right],\qquad \sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(r)}.\] \(\sigma\) (volatility) is in return units — "± how much in a typical year." In data use sample versions \(\hat\mu=\tfrac1T\sum_t r_t,\ \hat\sigma^2=\tfrac1{T-1}\sum_t (r_t-\hat\mu)^2\).
🟢 Example 1 · a coin-toss stock
Two fair coin flips: HH \(\to+40\%\), HT/TH \(\to+10\%\), TT \(\to-20\%\) (probabilities \(\tfrac14,\tfrac12,\tfrac14\)). Find \(\mu\) and \(\sigma\).

How to recognise: discrete outcomes with known probabilities → just apply the definitions. How to start: mean first, then square deviations from that mean. · 怎么认:离散、概率已知;怎么下手:先算均值,再对均值求平方偏差。

💡 Hint
Deviations from the mean are what the variance squares — not the raw returns.
✅ Solution
  1. \(\mu=\tfrac14(40)+\tfrac12(10)+\tfrac14(-20)=10\%\)
  2. deviations: \(+30,\ 0,\ -30\)
  3. \(\sigma^2=\tfrac14(30)^2+\tfrac12(0)^2+\tfrac14(-30)^2=450\ (\%^2)\)
  4. \(\sigma=\sqrt{450}=21.2\%\)
✅ \(\mu=10\%,\ \ \sigma=21.2\%\)

Sanity check: 21.2% ≈ the 19.7% historical SD of a diversified index; a single stock is usually far more volatile.

The two workhorse formulas · 两个主力公式

Portfolio mean & variance Weights \(w_i\) with \(\sum_i w_i=1\) (\(w_i<0\) = short), return \(r_P=\sum_i w_i r_i\): \[\mathbb{E}[r_P]=\sum_i w_i\,\mu_i,\qquad \mathrm{Var}(r_P)=\sum_i\sum_j w_i w_j\,\rho_{ij}\,\sigma_i\sigma_j.\] Two assets: \(\ \mathrm{Var}(r_P)=w_1^2\sigma_1^2+w_2^2\sigma_2^2+2w_1w_2\,\rho_{12}\,\sigma_1\sigma_2.\)
  • Mean is linear in weights; variance is quadratic and uses all \(\rho_{ij}\). · 均值线性、方差二次。
  • For \(0<w<1\): \(\sigma_P=w_1\sigma_1+w_2\sigma_2\) only if \(\rho_{12}=1\); for any \(\rho_{12}<1\) it is strictly smaller. · 只有完全正相关才等于加权平均,否则严格更小。
🟡 Example 2 · a 60/40 two-asset portfolio
Technology \(\mu_T=19\%,\sigma_T=18\%\); staples \(\mu_S=10\%,\sigma_S=13\%\); \(\rho=0.5\). Take \(w_T=0.6,\ w_S=0.4\). Find \(\mathbb{E}[r_P]\) and \(\sigma_P\).

How to recognise: two assets, weights given → plug into the two formulas. How to start: mean by weighted average; variance term-by-term, don't forget the cross term. · 怎么认:两资产+权重已知;怎么下手:均值加权,方差逐项算、别漏交叉项。

💡 Hint
The cross term is \(2w_Tw_S\rho\sigma_T\sigma_S\). Keep units in \(\%^2\), take the square root at the end.
✅ Solution
  1. \(\mathbb{E}[r_P]=0.6(19)+0.4(10)=15.4\%\)
  2. own terms: \(0.36(324)+0.16(169)=116.64+27.04\)
  3. cross term: \(2(0.6)(0.4)(0.5)(18)(13)=56.16\)
  4. \(\mathrm{Var}=199.8\ (\%^2)\Rightarrow\sigma_P=\sqrt{199.8}=14.1\%\)
✅ \(\mathbb{E}[r_P]=15.4\%,\ \ \sigma_P=14.1\%\)

Weighted average of the \(\sigma\)'s is \(0.6(18)+0.4(13)=16.0\%\); the portfolio's 14.1% sits below it. That gap is diversification, and it appears because \(\rho=0.5<1\).

Diversification: the result · 分散化的结论

  • Equal weights \(1/N\), each variance \(\sigma^2\), common pairwise correlation \(\rho\):
Diversification
\[\mathrm{Var}(r_P)=\underbrace{\tfrac1N\sigma^2}_{\text{idiosyncratic}\to 0}+\underbrace{\tfrac{N-1}{N}\rho\sigma^2}_{\text{systematic}\to\rho\sigma^2}\ \xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}\ \rho\sigma^2.\]

Derivation: \(\mathrm{Var}(r_P)=\tfrac1{N^2}\!\left[N\sigma^2+N(N-1)\rho\sigma^2\right]\) — \(N\) variance terms, \(N(N-1)\) covariance terms — divide through.

Key idea 关键 Diversification kills idiosyncratic risk for free; the systematic part \(\rho\sigma^2\) survives. If risk can be shed for nothing, the market will not pay you to bear it. · 特质风险可以免费分散掉;只有分散不掉的系统风险,市场才会给补偿。
PART 3 · slides 19–25

The frontier & two-fund separation

前沿、切点组合、两基金分离

Mean–variance investors trace a frontier (a "bullet"). Add a risk-free asset and the best menu is one straight line through the single tangency portfolio — so everyone holds the same risky portfolio, levered up or down. · 加入无风险资产后,最优菜单是过唯一切点组合的一条直线;于是人人持有同一个风险组合,只是杠杆不同。

Assumption (to be justified in L2) Mean–variance preferences: investors like higher \(\mathbb{E}[r_P]\), dislike higher \(\mathrm{Var}(r_P)\); nothing else about the distribution matters. Why only mean and variance (not skewness, crash risk)? L2 derives exactly when this is justified. · 均值—方差偏好:只在意均值和方差;为什么不管偏度?L2 会给出成立的确切条件。

Two stocks: the opportunity set · 机会集

Stock \(A\): \(\mu_A=9\%,\sigma_A=42\%\). Stock \(B\): \(\mu_B=6\%,\sigma_B=30\%\). \(\rho=0.30\).

\(w_A\)−0.2500.270.5011.25
\(\mathbb{E}[r_P]\) %5.36.06.87.59.09.8
\(\sigma_P\) %35.830.027.529.242.050.8
  • At \(w_A\approx0.27\): lower risk than either stock alone, mean in between — the bend of the "bullet". · 比单独持有任一只都更低风险。
  • \(w_A=1.25\) (short \(B\)): higher mean, much higher risk — short sales extend the set. · 做空会把机会集向两端拉长。
  • The curve bends left of the straight line joining \(A,B\): that bend is diversification. Upper limb = efficient (highest mean for given risk); nobody holds the lower limb. · 弯曲就是分散化;上半支才有效。
Many assets — the frontier
For each target mean \(\bar\mu\): \(\displaystyle\min_{w_1,\dots,w_N}\mathrm{Var}(r_P)\ \text{ s.t. }\ \sum_i w_i\mu_i=\bar\mu,\ \sum_i w_i=1.\) Tracing solutions over \(\bar\mu\) gives the minimum-variance frontier — a hyperbola in \((\sigma,\mu)\); its upper limb is the efficient frontier.
  • Every extra asset pushes the frontier northwest. Adding assets can never hurt (put zero weight on the newcomer); it strictly helps unless the new asset is already mean–variance spanned. · 多一个资产不会变差,通常变好。

Add a risk-free asset → a straight line · 加入无风险资产

Mix weight \(w\) in risky \(P\), \(1-w\) at \(r_f\):

Capital allocation line \[\mathbb{E}[r]=r_f+w\big(\mathbb{E}[r_P]-r_f\big),\qquad \sigma=|w|\,\sigma_P.\] Substituting \(w=\sigma/\sigma_P\) gives the line \(\ \mathbb{E}[r]=r_f+\dfrac{\mathbb{E}[r_P]-r_f}{\sigma_P}\,\sigma.\)
  • A straight line from \((0,r_f)\) through \((\sigma_P,\mathbb{E}[r_P])\). Slope = the Sharpe ratio \(\dfrac{\mathbb{E}[r_P]-r_f}{\sigma_P}\) — extra mean per unit of risk. · 斜率就是夏普比率。
  • \(w>1\): borrow at \(r_f\) and lever up — points beyond \(P\) on the same line. · 杠杆就是延长线。
Tangency portfolio & the CML Pivot the line up until it is tangent to the frontier at \(T\): \(T\) is the risky portfolio with the maximum Sharpe ratio. Everything on this (capital market) line dominates everything on the curve except \(T\) itself.
Theorem · two-fund separation
With a risk-free asset, every mean–variance investor holds a combination of just two funds: \(r_f\) and the tangency portfolio \(T\). Investors who agree on \(\mu_i,\sigma_i,\rho_{ij},r_f\) hold the same \(T\). Risk aversion sets only the mix, never the composition of \(T\).
  • Cautious: mostly \(r_f\), a little \(T\). Aggressive: levered \(T\). Nobody holds a different risky portfolio. · 保守的多拿无风险、激进的加杠杆,但风险组合都是同一个 \(T\)。
  • Your job, age and tastes affect how much risk you take, not which risky portfolio. This is the intellectual foundation of index funds — and the springboard to the CAPM. · 这就是指数基金的理论根基,也是通往 CAPM 的跳板。
PART 4 · slides 26–35

The CAPM

切点=市场 · beta · 证券市场线

If everyone holds the same \(T\) and markets clear, \(T\) must be the market \(M\). Then what an asset must earn depends only on its beta — its covariance with the market, not its own variance. · 人人持同一个 \(T\) 且市场出清 ⇒ \(T=M\);于是资产该赚多少只取决于它的 beta(与市场的协方差),与自身方差无关。

Equilibrium assumptions (stated honestly) all investors are MV optimizers over the same horizon; homogeneous expectations (agree on all \(\mu_i,\sigma_i,\rho_{ij}\)); frictionless markets (no taxes/costs, unlimited borrow/lend at \(r_f\), unlimited short sales). Strong — the value is the sharpness of the prediction; L5 confronts it with data. · 假设很强,但诚实摆出;模型的价值在于预测够锋利,L5 用数据检验。
Equilibrium: \(T=M\) By two-fund separation every investor's risky holdings are the same \(T\). Markets must clear — all shares held by someone — so the aggregate risky portfolio — the value-weighted market portfolio \(M\) — must equal \(T\). Prices adjust until the market portfolio is the tangency portfolio. · 价格会调整到市场组合就是切点组合。
Definition · beta \[\beta_i=\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(r_i,r_M)}{\mathrm{Var}(r_M)}=\rho_{iM}\,\frac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_M}.\] Regression form: \(r_i-r_f=\alpha_i+\beta_i(r_M-r_f)+\varepsilon_i\) — \(\beta_i\) is the slope. Market \(\beta_M=1\); cash \(\beta=0\); portfolio betas add linearly \(\beta_P=\sum_i w_i\beta_i\).
  • Beta is not relative volatility. A very volatile stock with low correlation has a small beta. · 高波动但低相关 ⇒ beta 反而小。

The two parts of risk · 风险的两部分

Variance decomposition Taking variances of the regression (with \(\varepsilon_i\perp r_M\)): \[\sigma_i^2=\underbrace{\beta_i^2\sigma_M^2}_{\text{systematic}}+\underbrace{\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon_i)}_{\text{idiosyncratic}},\qquad R^2=\frac{\beta_i^2\sigma_M^2}{\sigma_i^2}=\rho_{iM}^2.\]
🟡 Example 3 · splitting a stock's variance
\(\sigma_i=36\%,\ \sigma_M=18\%,\ \rho_{iM}=0.4\). Find \(\beta_i\) and the systematic share of variance.

How to recognise: you're given \(\sigma_i,\sigma_M,\rho\) → beta via \(\rho\,\sigma_i/\sigma_M\), systematic share is \(\rho^2\). · 怎么认:给了两个 σ 和 ρ;系统占比就是 \(\rho^2\)。

💡 Hint
\(\beta=\rho_{iM}\,\sigma_i/\sigma_M\); systematic variance \(=\beta^2\sigma_M^2\); divide by total \(\sigma_i^2\).
✅ Solution
  1. \(\beta_i=0.4\times 36/18=0.8\)
  2. systematic \(=0.8^2\times0.18^2=0.0207\)
  3. total \(=0.36^2=0.1296\)
  4. share \(=0.0207/0.1296=16\%\ (=\rho^2)\)
✅ \(\beta=0.8\); 16% systematic, 84% idiosyncratic — diversifiable.
Why beta, not variance? — marginal risk Your portfolio is (essentially) \(M\). Add a sliver \(\varepsilon\) of asset \(i\), funded from \(r_f\): \(\ \mathrm{Var}(r_M+\varepsilon r_i)\approx\mathrm{Var}(r_M)+2\varepsilon\,\mathrm{Cov}(r_i,r_M).\) The marginal risk is the covariance — idiosyncratic variance never appears. Only covariance can command a premium. · 在边际上,加一点资产 \(i\) 带来的风险增量就是协方差;特质方差根本不出现。
The CAPM (Sharpe 1964, Lintner 1965) \[\mathbb{E}[r_i]-r_f=\beta_i\big(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f\big)\quad\text{for every asset }i.\] \(\underbrace{\mathbb{E}[r_i]-r_f}_{\text{risk premium on }i}=\underbrace{\beta_i}_{\text{quantity of risk}}\times\underbrace{\big(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f\big)}_{\text{price of risk}}.\)
✅ Proof sketch (3 steps) · 证明梗概
  1. Form a portfolio: weight \(w\) in asset \(i\), \(1-w\) in \(M\); trace its curve in \((\sigma,\mu)\).
  2. At \(w=0\) the curve passes through \(M\) and lies below the CML on both sides (nothing beats the CML) ⇒ it is tangent there, so its slope at \(M\) equals the CML slope \(\dfrac{\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f}{\sigma_M}\).
  3. Compute the left side and rearrange — out drops the security market line. ∎

The security market line (SML) · 证券市场线

  • Plot expected return against beta (not \(\sigma\)). Line through \((0,r_f)\) and \((1,\mathbb{E}[r_M])\), slope \(=\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f\). · 横轴是 beta 不是 σ。
  • In equilibrium every asset and every portfolio — efficient or not — plots on the SML. · 均衡下所有资产都落在 SML 上。
⚠️ SML vs CML — do not confuse them
CMLSML

A single stock sits below the CML (it carries diversifiable risk) but on the SML (only its beta is priced). · 单只股票在 CML 下方、却在 SML 上。
Definition · alpha \[\alpha_i=\mathbb{E}[r_i]-\big[r_f+\beta_i(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)\big]\] the expected return beyond the CAPM benchmark. \(\alpha>0\): underpriced — buy. \(\alpha<0\): overpriced — sell/short. Under CAPM equilibrium every \(\alpha_i=0\). · 均衡下所有 alpha 都被竞争抹平。
PART 5 · slides 36–38

Using the CAPM — and the data

例题 · 用途 · 实证

The CAPM gives the right discount rate for a given risk. But in the data, high-beta portfolios don't earn what the SML promises — the dots are too flat. Hold that thought for L5. · CAPM 给出风险对应的折现率;但数据里高 beta 并没赚到 SML 承诺的收益——留到 L5。

🟢 Example 4 · expected return from beta
\(r_f=3\%,\ \mathbb{E}[r_M]=12\%,\ \beta=1.5\). Find \(\mathbb{E}[r]\).

How to start: plug straight into the SML \(\mathbb{E}[r]=r_f+\beta(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)\). · 直接代 SML。

✅ Solution
  1. \(\mathbb{E}[r]=3+1.5(12-3)\)
  2. \(=3+13.5\)
✅ \(\mathbb{E}[r]=16.5\%\)
🟡 Example 5 · back out the market premium
A \(\beta=0.75\) stock has expected excess return 6%. Find the market premium, then the total expected return of a \(\beta=1.3\) stock when \(r_f=2\%\).

How to recognise: they give you premium & beta, ask for the price of risk → invert the CAPM, then re-apply. · 怎么认:给溢价+beta 求风险价格;先反解再代回。

💡 Hint
\(\text{excess}=\beta\times(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)\). Solve for the bracket first.
✅ Solution
  1. \(6=0.75\times(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)\Rightarrow \mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f=8\%\)
  2. \(\beta=1.3\): excess \(=1.3\times8=10.4\%\)
  3. total \(=r_f+\text{excess}=2+10.4\)
✅ premium \(=8\%\); total expected return \(=12.4\%\)

What is the CAPM for? · CAPM 有什么用

  • Discount rates: valuing a project/stock needs an expected return matched to its risk — \(\bar r=r_f+\beta(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)\). Exactly the input L4's valuation formulas need. · 给估值提供折现率,正是 L4 要的输入。
  • Cost of capital: same formula from the firm's side. · 企业端就是资本成本。
  • Performance evaluation: skill is \(\alpha\), not raw return — levering beta is not skill. · 本事是 alpha,不是堆 beta。
  • Portfolio tilting: found \(\alpha>0\)? Overweight it — but recognise you're disagreeing with the market. · 你在跟市场对赌。
Does the data support it? Ten \(\beta\)-sorted US portfolios (1963–2026): the realised line is too flat — high-\(\beta\) portfolios do not earn what the SML (slope ≈ 7.2) promises. The empirical failure, and what to make of it, is Lecture 5. · 按 beta 排序的组合实际收益线太平,高 beta 没赚到承诺的钱;完整说法在 L5。

One-page recap

一页速查 · 截图这页

The formulas that must live in your hands

  • Variance / vol: \(\sigma^2=\mathbb{E}[(r-\mu)^2]\), \(\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2}\)
  • Portfolio: \(\mathbb{E}[r_P]=\sum_i w_i\mu_i\), \(\mathrm{Var}(r_P)=\sum_i\sum_j w_iw_j\rho_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j\)
  • Two assets: \(\mathrm{Var}=w_1^2\sigma_1^2+w_2^2\sigma_2^2+2w_1w_2\rho_{12}\sigma_1\sigma_2\)
  • Diversification: \(\mathrm{Var}(r_P)=\tfrac1N\sigma^2+\tfrac{N-1}{N}\rho\sigma^2\to\rho\sigma^2\)
  • Sharpe / CML slope: \(\dfrac{\mathbb{E}[r_P]-r_f}{\sigma_P}\)   CML: \(\mathbb{E}[r]=r_f+\tfrac{\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f}{\sigma_M}\sigma\)
  • Beta: \(\beta_i=\tfrac{\mathrm{Cov}(r_i,r_M)}{\mathrm{Var}(r_M)}=\rho_{iM}\tfrac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_M}\)
  • CAPM / SML: \(\mathbb{E}[r_i]-r_f=\beta_i(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)\)
  • Alpha: \(\alpha_i=\mathbb{E}[r_i]-[r_f+\beta_i(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f)]\)

Which line is which

  • CML — x-axis \(\sigma\); only efficient portfolios; slope = Sharpe of \(M\).
  • SML — x-axis \(\beta\); every asset; slope = \(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f\).
  • A single stock: below the CML, on the SML.
The spine of the lecture 一句话
  • Only systematic (undiversifiable) risk is priced; idiosyncratic risk earns nothing.
  • Everyone holds \(r_f+T\); equilibrium forces \(T=M\); the price of covariance is \(\mathbb{E}[r_M]-r_f\).
Two facts, restated Equities earn a large premium (≈8.8%) and are far riskier. The CAPM says the premium is pay for beta — but the data (flat line) already hints the story is incomplete. Next: why investors care about mean & variance (expected utility, L2).
